# Gestionale Template Skill

Reusable template for gyms, studios and sports associations.

Tenant configuration: brand, legal name, logo, colors, address, locale, expiry warning, membership plans and payment methods.

Core modules: Dashboard, Customers, Memberships, Payments, Expirations, Settings, Audit Log.

Rule: a new gym should be configuration/data, not a fork of business logic.

Non-negotiables: tenant isolation, server-side authorization, input validation, auditability, backups, automated tests and GDPR lifecycle before production.
